What this means for you as a buyer

You have real negotiating room. At ~5.8 months of inventory, neither buyers nor sellers hold all the leverage here — and the homes currently for sale have sat a median of 56 days, while the ones that actually sold went in just 24. That gap tells you a chunk of active listings are priced ahead of the market. Those are where your offer has the most pull.

But well-priced homes still move. The 50 recent sales closed at 95% of their original asking price in under a month. When the right home comes up priced correctly, you'll need to move quickly and offer smart — that's the difference between the two numbers, and it's exactly where Phil earns his keep.

This is acreage living. Lots run roughly an acre, homes are custom-built (2002–2027), and nearly 3 in 4 of the homes for sale have a private pool. Sizes range from cozy-for-the-area up to 9-bedroom estates, so the "median" hides a wide spread — the home that fits you could be well under, or well over, $1.45M.
