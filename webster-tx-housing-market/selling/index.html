Is it still a seller's market in Webster, TX?

Depends on your price. Under $300K it's a seller's market, from $400K–$500K it's a seller's market, and from $500K–$750K it's balanced.

What price will actually sell in Webster?

Homes that sold went under contract in a median of 20 days. Homes still for sale have sat 71 days. The typical seller closed at 98.1% of their original price, and 35 listings came off the market without selling.

Will I have to pay the buyer's closing costs in Webster?

Plan on it. 53% of Webster sales in the last six months included seller-paid closing costs or repairs. When sellers paid, the median was $10,000. Put it in your net sheet on day one so it's a plan, not a surprise.

How do I compete with the builder down the road?

51% of Webster sales were new construction. Builders win on new finishes and rate buydowns. You can win on a lower tax rate, which saves a buyer about $234 a month on a $400K home. Put that number in your marketing.
