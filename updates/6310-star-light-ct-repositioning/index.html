Step 6 · Learn from the listings that did not sell

The homes that failed made the same few mistakes.

  • They waited. 29 of the 53 failed listings never changed their price. They expired at the number they started with.
  • They cut in small steps. Eleven came back on the market at a lower price. All eleven are still for sale.
  • They ended up lower anyway. Five later sold. They closed at a median of 91% of their first asking price.

The homes that met the market early kept the most money. Waiting has its own cost too. Each month is another $3,244 mortgage payment, and only about $1,400 of it comes back to you as equity.
