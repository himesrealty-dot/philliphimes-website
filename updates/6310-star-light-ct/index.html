What has happened since our last update

Two homes in Benders Landing went under contract. That is the headline, and it is genuinely good news, because it means buyers are active and writing offers in your neighborhood right now.

27498 S Lazy Meadow Way Under contract

Right around the corner. Listed 7 July at $1,495,000, lowered to $1,450,000 on 20 July, and under contract on 7 August.

4223 Maple Rapids Lane Option pending

Listed 26 June at $1,750,000. It did not sell, came off the market on 30 July, went back on the same day at $1,695,000, and was under contract fifteen days later.

One important caveat. Neither home has closed yet, so we do not know what either one will actually sell for. The contract price stays private until funding. What we do know is the price each seller had to be at before a buyer would write. In both cases that was after a reduction, not before one.

You also have age on your side. Yours was built in 2021. Lazy Meadow is 2015, Maple Rapids is 2014. Newer construction, newer mechanicals, newer everything.

So this is a real trade rather than a shortfall. They have the extra room to spread out. You have the newer home and the specialty spaces nobody else in the comp set can match. The catch is that more buyers at this price are shopping for a media room than for a wine room, and that is exactly why the price has to do more of the work for us.

The opening weekend was strong. Eleven people came through the open house, and we booked five private showings on top of that. Three of those buyers walked the home. One was canceled by the agent, and one on 25 July never got inside because the gate code failed and nobody could reach me. That one still bothers me and I own it.

Your last showing was 7 August. That is fourteen days ago.

Visibility is not the problem. Your listing has been put in front of buyers 12,360 times on Homes.com, it held a top-of-search position 830 times, and it is syndicated to 45 sites. Four people saved it and two sent an inquiry.

So people are finding it and they are looking at it. What has stopped is people coming to see it. Fourteen days of quiet, in the same two weeks that two neighbors went under contract, is the clearest signal we have.

For most buyers they will not.

  • Calling every agent who has walked a buyer through, more than once. That is how I got Paul back to the table.
  • Paul is coming back Saturday at 1:30. You will see the appointment come through. His hesitation has been price, and a second visit is the one that counts.
  • Chasing the JLA Realty agent whose buyer called the home beautiful and kept it on their list.
  • Making certain the gate access failure from 25 July cannot happen to anyone else.

My recommendation

$1,325,000

A reduction of $100,000, or 7.0%. That puts you at $343 a square foot.

That number is not a guess. It lands between the two homes buyers are actually choosing right now, Balsam Fir at $345 and Lazy Meadow at $335 a square foot. It moves you from the most expensive home in the comparison to a competitive one, and it puts real daylight between you and Lazy Meadow instead of $25,000.

It also answers the size question. If a buyer is choosing between your 3,862 square feet and Lazy Meadow’s 4,329 with a media room, the honest way to win that buyer is to be clearly better value, not nearly the same price.

I would rather make one clean move than chase the market down twenty five thousand at a time. Small cuts keep you above the buyers the whole way down and they make a listing look tired. Both homes that just went under contract moved once, and then found their buyer.

I am not going to tell Paul we are considering anything before Saturday. I want to see what he does at the current price first.

Once we are at $1,325,000, I think we hold there. I am not seeing prices falling across the board in Benders Landing. What I am seeing is homes that opened high coming down to meet the market and then going under contract quickly. That is a pattern we can use rather than fear.
