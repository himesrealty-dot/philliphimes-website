The pool

Every sale in this set has a pool. 27418 does not.

At this price, in this neighborhood, a private pool is expected. The six closest sales all have one, and so does every resale between 6,000 and 7,500 square feet currently for sale in Benders Landing Estates. A buyer here is weighing this house against homes that already have the backyard finished.

The grid deducts $75,000 for it. That is a conservative figure: it is what the pool adds to a sale price, not what one costs to design, permit and build, and it puts no value on the months a buyer spends living through construction. I made no deduction for the acre being unfenced.

Credit where it is due
27418 is newer than five of the six sales, and the grid adds up to $45,000 for that. The casita, theater and game room are all inside the 6,675 square feet it is credited for, and the generator is added on top.

Method

Why the median and not the average

28007 Whispering Maple Way closed at $2,400,000, above its asking price, the day it was listed. It sits on two acres, and a zero-day sale above list means something about that house made a buyer move immediately. Adjusted, it still lands at $2,251,228, more than $450,000 above any other sale in the set. Averaging would let that one sale lift the entire number.

One sale moves the average by $103,567. The median keeps that sale in the set and lets it set the top of the range, without dragging the middle upward. The median lands within $3,000 of the average without it, which tells us the other five sales agree with each other.

The same logic is why I did not price this home off the neighborhood’s price per square foot. Four homes over 6,000 square feet are listed at $280 to $296 a foot today, and they have been sitting for 66 to 432 days. Buyers are not paying those numbers for large homes here.

Where that leaves us
