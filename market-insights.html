Friendswood Market Update — June 2026

The fastest market in the Bay Area: 20 days on market at a $430K median. Avalon, West Ranch, Heritage Park, and the neighborhoods driving it.

Pearland Market Update — June 2026

636 sales at a $380K median — but a 43.5% listing failure rate, the highest in the area. Shadow Creek Ranch, Silverlake, and what pricing right means here.
🚀

Clear Lake Market Update — June 2026

The tightest inventory of the four markets: 3.0 months of supply, 98.6% close-to-list. Bay Oaks, Bay Forest, Brookwood, and more.
🌿

Dickinson Market Update — June 2026

212 homes sold at a $294,500 median — but 52% of listings failed to sell, the highest fail rate of any market I track. Bay Colony, Pedregal, Peacock Isle, and more.
🤠

Manvel Market Update — June 2026

A new-construction market: 406 sales at a $479K median, but a long 67 days on market and 5.7 months of supply. Meridiana, Pomona, Valencia, and more.
