Kemah buyer guide · November 2026

Is it a buyer's market in Kemah, TX?

Overall, Kemah has 4.8 months of supply, a balanced market. From $300K–$400K, sellers still have the edge and well-priced homes move fast. Your leverage comes from homes that have been sitting, not from the price range.

Updated November 2026 · Source: HAR MLS, City of Kemah, TX, single-family sales May 1 – Oct 31, 2026 (72 sales)

How much can I negotiate on a home in Kemah?

It depends on how long the home has been sitting. 83% of homes sold below their original price. The longer a home sat, the bigger the discount the seller accepted.

Will the seller pay my closing costs in Kemah?

Very likely, if you ask the right way. 72% of Kemah buyers in the last six months got seller-paid closing costs or repairs, up from 66% a year ago. When sellers paid, the median was $11,576. That's cash that stays in your pocket at closing.

New build or resale: which costs less each month?

Don't compare sticker prices; compare payments. New homes here often carry a higher tax rate: about $344 more a month on a $400K home. A builder's rate buydown can make up for it, or not. Run both before you fall in love.
