The 30-Day Plan to Prepare Your Home to Sell

The exact prep sequence that gets homes sold faster and for more — room by room, one day at a time. Tick each day off as you go; the page remembers where you got to.

Start with Day 1 → Get text check-ins
0 of 30 done

Don't just list your home without any advance preparation. A few minor touch-ups go a long way toward making a favourable impression on buyers — and perhaps cinching a deal. Put your buyer's hat on and walk through your home like it's the first time. Make notes on what you, as a buyer, would notice, then repair or replace those things.

I've built this as a 30-day plan so you don't get overwhelmed, and can take it one day at a time. Reach out any time with questions — and know that we'll be checking in with you throughout the 30 days.

— Phil
