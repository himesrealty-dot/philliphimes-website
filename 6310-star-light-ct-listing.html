A Gated One-Acre Estate in Benders Landing

Set behind a private gated drive on a beautifully landscaped one-acre lot, this 2021-built contemporary estate in coveted Benders Landing Estates spans 3,862 square feet of refined, single-story living — four bedrooms and four-and-a-half baths designed for both grand entertaining and everyday comfort.

The open floor plan centers on a gourmet island kitchen that flows into soaring living spaces, while two standout retreats set this home apart: a luxurious wine cellar and a dedicated cigar lounge with its own ventilation system. Out back, a resort-style setting delivers a sparkling pool and spa, a sunken fire pit, and generous space to relax under the pines.

A three-car garage, gated driveway, and a peaceful estate setting complete the package — all with easy access to The Woodlands and the Grand Parkway (99). This is elevated Bay Area–to–Woodlands living on a rare full acre.
