Getting in Touch, Answered

A few quick things before you reach out.

What's the best way to reach Phil?
Call, email, or send a message with the form on this page — whatever's easiest for you. Phil handles his own inquiries, so you're talking to him, not a call center.
How quickly will Phil get back to me?
Usually within one business day, and often sooner. If your timing is tight, mention it in your message and he'll make you a priority.
Is there any cost or obligation to reach out?
None at all. The first conversation is free, and there's no pressure to buy or list — it's just a chance to get your questions answered.
Can I reach out if I'm only starting to think about buying or selling?
Absolutely — earlier is better. Even if you're months out, an early conversation lets Phil help you plan the smart moves before you need to make them.
