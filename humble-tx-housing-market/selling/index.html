Is it still a seller's market in Humble, TX?

Depends on your price. Under $300K it's balanced, from $300K–$400K buyers are in charge, and from $400K–$600K it's balanced.

What price will actually sell in Humble?

Homes that sold went under contract in a median of 27 days. Homes still for sale have sat 53 days. The typical seller closed at 97.3% of their original price, and 658 listings came off the market without selling.

Will I have to pay the buyer's closing costs in Humble?

Plan on it. 69% of Humble sales in the last six months included seller-paid closing costs or repairs, up from 60% a year ago. When sellers paid, the median was $7,755. Put it in your net sheet on day one so it's a plan, not a surprise.

How do I compete with the builder down the road?

7% of Humble sales were new construction. Builders win on new finishes and rate buydowns. You can win on a lower tax rate, which saves a buyer about $277 a month on a $400K home. Put that number in your marketing.
