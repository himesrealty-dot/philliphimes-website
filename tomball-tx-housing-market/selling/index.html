Is it still a seller's market in Tomball, TX?

Depends on your price. Under $300K it's a seller's market, from $300K–$1M it's balanced, and above $1M buyers are in charge.

Will I have to pay the buyer's closing costs in Tomball?

Plan on it. 61% of Tomball sales in the last six months included seller-paid closing costs or repairs, up from 51% a year ago. When sellers paid, the median was $7,812. Put it in your net sheet on day one so it's a plan, not a surprise.

How do I compete with the builder down the road?

24% of Tomball sales were new construction. Builders win on new finishes and rate buydowns. You can win on a lower tax rate, which saves a buyer about $234 a month on a $400K home. Put that number in your marketing.
