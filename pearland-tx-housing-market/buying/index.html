Pearland buyer guide · November 2026

Is it a buyer's market in Pearland, TX?

Overall, Pearland has 3.2 months of supply, a seller's market. Under $600K, sellers still have the edge and well-priced homes move fast. Your leverage grows from $600K–$800K.

Updated November 2026 · Source: HAR MLS, City of Pearland, TX, single-family sales May 1 – Oct 31, 2026 (724 sales)

How much can I negotiate on a home in Pearland?

It depends on how long the home has been sitting. 71% of homes sold below their original price. The longer a home sat, the bigger the discount the seller accepted.

Will the seller pay my closing costs in Pearland?

Very likely, if you ask the right way. 62% of Pearland buyers in the last six months got seller-paid closing costs or repairs, up from 54% a year ago. When sellers paid, the median was $8,000. That's cash that stays in your pocket at closing.

New build or resale: which costs less each month?

Don't compare sticker prices; compare payments. New homes here often carry a higher tax rate: about $304 more a month on a $400K home. A builder's rate buydown can make up for it, or not. Run both before you fall in love.
