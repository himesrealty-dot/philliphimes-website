Homes that sold went under contract in a median of 14 days. Homes still for sale have sat 43 days. The typical seller closed at 97.3% of their original price, and 486 listings came off the market without selling.

Will I have to pay the buyer's closing costs in The Woodlands?

Plan on it. 42% of The Woodlands sales in the last six months included seller-paid closing costs or repairs, down from 44% a year ago. When sellers paid, the median was $5,175. Put it in your net sheet on day one so it's a plan, not a surprise.
