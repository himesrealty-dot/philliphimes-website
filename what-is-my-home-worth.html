Home Value Questions, Answered

Quick answers before you request your TruMarket™ report.

How is this different from a Zestimate?
A Zestimate is an algorithm guessing from a distance. Phil's TruMarket™ report is a real person pulling the actual comparable sales on your street and factoring in your home's condition and updates — the number a buyer would really pay.
Is the home value really free?
Yes — completely free, with no obligation to list. It's just a straight answer about what your home is worth today.
What do you need from me to give a value?
Your address and a few basics to start. The more Phil knows about your updates and improvements, the sharper the number gets.
Does getting a value mean I have to sell?
Not at all. Plenty of homeowners request a value just to know where they stand. There's no pressure and no obligation.
