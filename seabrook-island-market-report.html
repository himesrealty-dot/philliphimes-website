What this means for you as a buyer

Decide which Seabrook Island you're buying. The "median" ($525K) hides two very different markets: interior homes around $480K, and canal-front homes with a boat slip around $1.22M. Nearly all of that gap is buying direct water access — a boat house and lift out your back door. Know which tier fits you before you start, because the comps and the negotiation are completely different between them.

Move decisively — this is a fairly tight market. At ~3.9 months of inventory, homes are selling in a median of 29 days at 96% of original list. When a home is priced right, it goes quickly. The active listings sitting a median of 77 days are the ones testing higher prices — and that's where your offer has the most room.

Waterfront is scarce — be ready. Of the six homes for sale right now, only one is on the water. Canal-front homes here don't come up often, and when the right one does, you'll want to already have Phil lined up so you can move before someone else does.
